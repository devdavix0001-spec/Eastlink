Eastlink & Partners Developers LLC is a real estate development firm based in Kwabenya, Ghana, bringing property and construction services together under one roof.

Our work spans development, brokerage and marketing, architectural planning, building and construction, project management, and interior design. We bring together capabilities in construction, architecture, interior design, quantity surveying, project management and property law to plan and deliver solutions around each client’s needs.

Quality, creativity and thoughtful planning guide our approach.
